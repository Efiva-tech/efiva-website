What you're paying for

The monthly price runs it. The one-time fee shapes it.

Questions owners ask

How is the price decided?

By the number of people who will use Efiva, not your total headcount. A company of 200 employees where 50 people use the system is priced at 50. If you sit just above a band, say 32 users, tell us and we'll price it fairly instead of moving you to the next band. For 100+ users, talk to us.

What is the audit?

A basic understanding of how your company works: who reports to whom, how work is assigned and followed up. We do it with every company, on every plan, so your hierarchy and people are set up properly.

What is a Systems Design?

The deeper work in the Custom plan. We study your whole company, tell you what is broken and what to change, re-engineer your workflow, and build the software around it.

What does the customization fee cover?

Standard has none. Tailored (₹20,000) covers building the changes and features your company needs into the system. Custom (₹50,000) covers the full Systems Design: studying your company, redesigning the workflow, and building the software around it. It is paid once, and agreed with you before we start.

What's in the free week?

After a short demo call, your team gets a full week on a working copy of the system. No card, no commitment. We agree a start date on the call, and the test data is cleared when the week ends.

Is there a lock-in?

No. Plans are billed monthly or yearly. Standard has no customization fee; Tailored and Custom have a one-time customization fee, listed above.

Where does our data live?

Every company gets its own separate database and its own instance, on a subdomain of your company's or ours. Nothing is shared with other clients.

Can you visit us on site?

Our work is online for now. On-site visits can be arranged, with travel and stay covered by you.

Can we change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade at any time. The customization fee is one-time.
