The product

Tasks, the daily plan and the record, in one system.

Work is assigned or declared, planned into the day, completed, approved and rolled up to the owner. Every step is on record, so your team spends its time on the work, not on updates.

Tasks

Assigned from above, declared from within.

Managers assign tasks down the chain, and people declare their own. Everything lands in one Tasks area, with My Tasks, Team Tasks and an Approval Queue.

  • Assign to anyone below you
  • Declare your own tasks
  • Every task shows who it came from

Daily Planning

Plan the day in time slots.

Each person places their tasks into time slots and submits the day when they are done. If it isn't submitted, the day closes by itself at a time your company sets.

  • A time slot for every task
  • Submit day, or let it close automatically
  • Once closed, entries are read-only

Approvals

Completed by the employee. Done when the manager approves.

Two steps keep the record honest. The employee marks a task Completed, and the manager approves it as Done from the Approval Queue.

  • A queue of completed work waiting for approval
  • Late work is marked as such, not hidden
  • Every status has one meaning

Dashboard

The owner sees it without asking.

  • Every department with its own completion
  • Open a department to see each person

Every change, on record.

Each task keeps a Status History and a Change Log, so you can see who changed what and when.

  • Who assigned it, completed it and approved it
  • Changes to due dates and priorities are logged
  • Nothing is edited quietly
