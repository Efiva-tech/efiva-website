About Efiva

We make the record nobody can argue with.

Efiva is a task and commitment management system for growing companies.

Our vision

To set the standard for accountability in growing companies: every commitment recorded, every outcome visible, and leadership able to rely on the facts.

Our mission

To give every company one dependable system for daily planning, accountability and visibility, built around how it actually operates and backed by direct, personal support.

Our story

It started with a question nobody could answer.

Most companies run on follow-ups. Someone asks, someone replies, and weeks later nobody can be sure what was promised, or when.

So we built a system for ourselves. Every person plans the day, every commitment is recorded, and the day locks so the record can't be quietly changed.

We ran it every day in a company of 100+ people and improved it from what the team actually needed. When it held up, we opened it to others. That became Efiva.
